esFormLab

Running form analysis in minutes

No specialist equipment. No lab visit. Three short clips from your phone is all you need.

01

Record 3 short clips

Capture a side, front, and back view of your stride — treadmill or outdoor track, phone camera is fine. MP4, MOV, or WebM. Your videos are analyzed in your browser and stay on your device; only still frames are sent when you generate a PDF report.

02

9 phases detected

The tool finds the key moments in your stride: foot strike and toe-off on each side and mid-stance from the side view, plus left and right mid-stance from the front and back views.

03

Review and refine

Each phase shows a skeleton overlay with angle arcs and a detection-quality readout. Select frame by frame to land on the right moment, with plain-language coaching guidance built in.

04

Complete your report

Upload & scan at no cost on a free account, then unlock the running form report with a one-time pass — detected issues like overstriding, hip drop, and knee valgus with severity grading and coaching cues, plus joint angles across all 9 phases graded against elite reference ranges and a downloadable PDF.

◆ Tailored to your body

Your stride is graded against runners of the same gender — not blended into a one-size-fits-all average.

Male and female runners differ meaningfully in average gait mechanics: stride length, hip drop, foot-strike pattern, knee mechanics, and arm carriage all trend differently. esFormLab grades your angles against a reference dataset built from runners of the same gender, so the bell curves and severity scores you see are calibrated to your body — not averaged across everyone.

Our philosophy

Built on science.
Designed for real athletes.

We build tools that translate lab-grade physiology into actionable insight — without requiring a lab, a team of scientists, or an elite contract.

Data without context is noise. Every number we report comes with an explanation of what it means for your performance and how to act on it.

Physiology is individual. Reference ranges are starting points. The goal is to build a picture of your own body over time — not compare you to an abstract average.

Accessibility matters. Lab-grade analysis shouldn't require a lab. We're building tools that bring the science to any athlete, anywhere, at any level.
